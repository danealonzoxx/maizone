About

Maizone Events Management Services

We are an event production and technical services company based in Manila, formed in November 2025. Our work so far has been civic and institutional programmes — an awards night in a heritage theater, a commemorative ceremony in a public plaza, a public wedding ceremony under canopy, and a costume competition in a covered court.

Equipment is sourced through suppliers we work with regularly and specified to each event, then installed, tested and operated by our own crew. We handle full production, or the technical side alongside your own organiser.
